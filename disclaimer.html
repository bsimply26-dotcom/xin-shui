Nature of the practice

Xin Shui offers self-reflection sessions drawn from a classical framework for understanding personal tendencies, strengths, and rhythms. Sessions are intended to support self-understanding and considered decision-making. They describe patterns already present in a person's nature and are offered as a space for reflection.

Not professional advice

Sessions and any written profile are provided for personal reflection and wellbeing only. They are not medical, psychological, therapeutic, financial, legal, or predictive advice, and are not a substitute for guidance from a qualified professional. If you have concerns about your health, finances, or legal position, please consult an appropriately licensed practitioner.

No promised outcomes

The sessions explore patterns already present and support personal reflection. We make no promise or guarantee of any particular result. Any decisions or actions taken following a session remain entirely the client's own responsibility.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. A client may pause, redirect, or end a session at any point. Sessions are available to adults.

Privacy

Information shared before and during a session is held in confidence and handled in line with our Privacy Notice.
