Xin Shui is a reflective practice built on the Five Elements and the Four Pillars, two frameworks from the classical Chinese tradition. We use them as a structured way to describe your natural tendencies, so you have clearer language for how you work, rest and relate to others.

You share your date, time and place of birth before we meet. Your practitioner prepares your profile, then walks you through it in a private conversation, and you leave with a short set of practices to try. Several formats are available. Selected formats also include a written profile to keep, and we will help you choose the one that suits you when you enquire.
